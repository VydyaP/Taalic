Delete Your Account & Data

Keerthana Collection

If you'd like your account and associated personal data removed from Keerthana Collection, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to vydyanpandithtv@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the Google account email address you signed in with.
  3. You'll receive a confirmation once the request has been processed, typically within 7 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record: your name, email address, and profile photo association are removed from Firebase Authentication.

What's retained

Keerthana Collection is a single shared collection editable by any signed-in account, rather than separate per-user data — keerthana entries (song details, lyrics, notation files) aren't individually attributed to the account that added them, so they aren't deleted as part of an account-deletion request. If you'd like specific content removed from the collection, mention that separately in your email and it will be handled on request.

Retention period

Account records are deleted within 7 days of a verified request. No backup copies of the deleted account record are retained beyond routine infrastructure backups, which are cycled out within 30 days.
