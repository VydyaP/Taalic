Privacy Policy

Keerthana Collection — last updated September 7, 2026

Keerthana Collection ("the app") is a personal application for managing a collection of Carnatic music compositions. This page explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Data we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo via Firebase Authentication, used only to identify your account and control access to the app.
  • Content you add: keerthana entries (song names, ragas, talas, composers, deities, lyrics, meanings) and any notation files you upload are stored in Google Firestore and Firebase Storage, associated with your account.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't collect data beyond what's described above.

Data storage

All data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage). Firebase's own privacy and security practices apply to how data is stored and transmitted; see Firebase's privacy policy for details.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact the developer at the email associated with this app's Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Changes will be reflected on this page.
